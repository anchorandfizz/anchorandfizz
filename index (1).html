Is this part of DoorDash?

Nope. Order through DoorDash or your usual delivery app; GateRun is a separate student-run handoff from the campus gate to your dorm.

Who is touching my food?

A fellow student runner picks up the sealed bag using the name on your order. They carry it; they do not open it.

Do I need another app?

No. The order button opens one quick form. Fill it out, then get back to whatever you were avoiding the walk for.

Where do I meet my runner after 10pm?

Usually in your dorm lobby. Residence hall access is limited after 10pm, so your runner will text when they arrive and meet you downstairs. If your runner lives in your hall, they can still bring it to your door.

Can I use it for a group order?

Absolutely. One gate pickup, one dorm drop, one $4 fee. Splitting that with your floor is elite economics.
