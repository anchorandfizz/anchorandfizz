Privacy Policy

The short version: you tell us where to bring your food, we bring it, and then we forget about it. We don't sell anything, we don't advertise to you, and we're not building a data empire — we're a student-run delivery handoff.

What we collect

When you fill out the order form, you give us:

  • your name
  • your dorm and room number
  • your phone number (so your runner can text you)
  • your food's gate ETA

That's the whole list. If you didn't type it, we don't have it.

What we use it for

Exactly one thing: getting your food from the Bathgate Gate to you. Your runner sees your name, room, and phone number so the handoff happens. Nobody else gets it — no advertisers, no data brokers, no marketing lists. We will never sell your info, and we will never use it for anything except delivering your order.

Who else touches it

Two companies handle our tech, so your details pass through their servers:

  • Yandex Forms runs our order form. Their privacy policy is at yandex.com/legal/privacy.
  • GitHub hosts this website. Their policy is at docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies.

We don't control what they do with data on their end — that's on them, and their policies apply there. Everything under our control follows the rules above.

How long we keep it

Order records get deleted after the semester ends. If you want yours gone sooner, DM us at @gate.run on Instagram and it's done.

Who we are

GateRun is run by Fordham students, not a company. We're the ones reading your DMs.
