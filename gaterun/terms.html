Terms of Service

What GateRun is

We're a student-run courier for the last leg of your delivery: from the Bathgate Gate to your dorm. That's it. We are not DoorDash, Uber Eats, or any delivery app — you order through your usual app, and we handle the campus gate → your door part. We are also not a restaurant, and we never touch open food: your runner picks up the sealed bag using the name on your order and carries it as-is.

The fee

$4 flat per order. One pickup at the gate, one drop at your dorm, one fee — even for group orders. The $4 covers the runner's trip; any tip you give goes straight to your runner.

Delivery rules

Before 10pm, we come to your door. After 10pm, meet us in your lobby (dorm access is limited at night, so your runner will text when they arrive) — unless your runner lives in your hall, in which case door delivery still works.

Missed handoffs

Your runner texts you twice at the meet point. If you don't come, your food is just dropped off.

Your order, your restaurant

Food quality, allergies, missing items, refunds — all of that lives with the restaurant and the app you ordered through, not with us. We're the courier, not the kitchen.

Availability

Orders go out when runners are on. Late-night room delivery (after 10pm, straight to your door) only happens when a runner who lives in your building is available; otherwise it's lobby meetup.

The obvious stuff

Don't use GateRun for anything that isn't food, don't lie on the order form, and treat your runner like a person. If something goes wrong, DM @gate.run and we'll sort it out.

The legal bit, briefly

GateRun is a student side hustle, not a corporation. To the fullest extent the law allows, we're not liable for the food itself, for anything the delivery app does, or for acts of weather, Fordham security policy, or your dorm's lobby furniture. Our liability for any one order is capped at the $4 fee for that order.
